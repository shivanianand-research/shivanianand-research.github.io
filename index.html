Research Interests

My work focuses on the intersection of cybersecurity, identity systems, cloud architectures, and intelligent automation. I am particularly interested in how emerging technologies can strengthen security, improve resilience, and support large‑scale digital transformation.

Core Areas of Interest

  • Cybersecurity & Zero Trust Architectures — secure design, threat modeling, and modern defense strategies.
  • Identity & Access Management (IAM) — authentication, authorization, governance, and risk‑based access.
  • Cloud Security — securing multi‑cloud environments, workload protection, and compliance automation.
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — AI, ML, anomaly detection, behavioral analytics, and intelligent threat response.
  • Data Analytics — using data‑driven insights to improve security posture and operational efficiency.
  • Emerging Technologies — AI‑driven security, privacy‑enhancing technologies, and secure digital identity ecosystems.

Current Focus

I am currently exploring how machine learning and identity systems can be combined to create adaptive, context‑aware security controls that reduce risk while improving user experience.
